\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
\textit{Structural interpretation.}
The graphs lack semantic link types and intended-route annotations. Degree and
topology remain influential; pairwise associations do not establish incremental
information after joint controls. Baseline edge energy neither guarantees nor is required for a response;
finite changes do not isolate individual links' causal effects.

\textit{Sampling and refinement design.}
Corpora use heterogeneous extraction surfaces without population sampling.
Attribution covers selected triangles on three anchors; exposure uses dependent
variants and an automatic support proxy. Refinement changes text, pages and links
together, with unequal admissions and one realization per arm on one corpus.
LT/R0 specify instructions, not validated structural classes; question resampling
does not estimate editor variability.

\textit{Outcome validity.}
LT optimality is unestablished, and factoring content may add reading steps.
Judge consistency (\hyperref[sec:review-appendix]{Appendix}), retry selection and
uncertain support constrain outcomes. Sham contrasts neither establish
equivalence nor bound judge noise. Task ceilings and prior knowledge remain
untested explanations.
